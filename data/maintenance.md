# Robot Maintenance Guide

## 📅 Maintenance Schedule

To keep your Spot Robot in top operating condition, follow this maintenance schedule:

- **Every 2 months** → Perform full **routine maintenance** as outlined in the checklist.
- **Immediately after any falls or impacts** → Inspect for internal and external damage, loose components, and sensor misalignment.
- **Anytime performance metrics degrade** → Investigate for signs of mechanical wear, dirt buildup, or calibration drift.

> Tip: Logging maintenance events helps identify recurring issues and track Spot’s health over time.

# Maintenance Procedure

Regular maintenance is essential to ensure **optimal performance**, **minimize downtime**, and **extend the lifespan** of your Spot Robot. This is especially important in **harsh environments** such as construction sites, oil and gas facilities, or underground mining areas, where dust and debris are common.

**Accumulated dust can impair fans, motors, gears, and sensors**, leading to overheating, operational faults, or long-term damage. To avoid these issues, perform the following routine checks:

## ✅ Maintenance Checklist

- [ ]  Clean all **sensors and cameras**
- [ ]  Inspect **joint actuators** for wear or abnormal movement
- [ ]  Check for **loose hardware or electrical connections**
- [ ]  Verify that **all covers and panels are securely fastened**

# ⚠️ General Rules

- Work in a clean, dust-free environment if possible.
- Never apply liquid directly onto the lens or sensor.
- Always use a **gentle touch** — pressing too hard can damage coatings or elements.

---

### 🧼 Step-by-Step: Lenses (External Glass Surfaces)

### 1. **Blow Away Dust**

- Use **compressed air** (short, controlled bursts) to remove loose dust.
- Hold the can upright to avoid releasing propellant.

### 2. **Inspect the Lens**

- Use angled light to spot smudges, oils, or residue.

### 3. **Dry Wipe (if light cleaning needed)**

- Use a clean **microfiber cloth**.
- Gently wipe in circular motions from center outward.

### 4. **Wet Wipe (if smudges or oils are present)**

- Lightly dampen a **microfiber cloth** or **lens-safe swab** with:
    - **Zweiss Lens Cleaner** (preferred for coated optics), **or**
    - A 50/50 mix of **isopropyl alcohol (IPA)** and distilled water (if Zweiss not available).
- Wipe gently in circular motion, one pass per cloth area.

### 5. **Final Buff (Optional)**

- Use a clean, dry microfiber cloth to polish out any streaks.

---

### 🧽 Step-by-Step: Sensors (e.g., IR, Depth, or Optical)

### 1. **Blow Off Debris**

- Use **compressed air** to dislodge loose dust — critical before touching.

### 2. **Light Surface Wipe**

- Use a **dry swab or microfiber cloth** for minimal surface dust.
- Avoid full wipes unless necessary.

### 3. **Wet Cleaning (if needed)**

- Dampen a **sensor-safe swab** (small tip, like PEC-PAD or equivalent) with IPA/distilled water mix or Zweiss.
- Wipe *lightly* across the sensor surface in one direction.
- Use a fresh swab for each pass if needed.

## 🔧 Internal Access: Spot Robot Panel Removal and Inspection

### **Step 1: Panel Removal**

- Position Spot on its side to ensure stable and easy access to the panel.
- Locate the panel secured with **four Allen screws**.
- **Important:** Allen screw lengths may vary, especially around vent areas.
    
    → As you remove each screw, **note its original position** to ensure correct reassembly.
    
- Using the appropriate Allen key, carefully remove all four screws.
- Gently lift off the panel, avoiding any tugging or forcing.
- Visually inspect the exposed area for **dust, dirt, or debris buildup**.

### **Step 2: Opposite Side Panel Removal and Inspection**

- Carefully reposition Spot to access the opposite side panel.
- Repeat the **removal and inspection process** as described in Step 1.
- As before, note the **screw positions and sizes** during disassembly.
- Visually inspect the interior for **dust, dirt, or debris**, ensuring both sides are cleaned thoroughly for optimal performance.

![IMG_7165.HEIC.heic](Robot%20Maintenance%20Guide%201e532e888e42800897a7dbbe6ba5be7b/IMG_7165.HEIC.heic)

### **Step 3: Internal Cleaning (Preferred: Blower Method)**

- Begin by using a **powered leaf blower** to clear dust and debris from internal components:
    - Direct airflow **downward and away** from computers and sensitive electronics.
    - Avoid aiming air directly into ports, vents, or circuit boards to prevent pushing dust deeper inside.
    - Use **short, controlled bursts** for better precision and to avoid damage.
- After blowing out loose debris, use a **microfiber cloth** to gently wipe down any remaining buildup on **fans, motors, and gears**.
    - Always wipe in a **downward motion** to minimize the risk of driving debris into the system.
    

![IMG_7166.HEIC.png](Robot%20Maintenance%20Guide%201e532e888e42800897a7dbbe6ba5be7b/IMG_7166.HEIC.png)

### **Step 4: Reassembly and Calibration**

- Carefully **reinstall all panels**, ensuring each is properly aligned.
- **Reinsert the screws** in their original positions, matching the correct lengths.
    
    → Tighten each screw securely, but avoid over-torquing to prevent damage.
    
- Perform a final check to confirm there are **no loose parts or tools left inside**.
- Power on the Spot robot and run a **calibration and joint offset test** using the Spot software interface.
    - Verify that all joints move smoothly and respond correctly.
    - Address any irregularities before field use to ensure safe and optimal performance.

# Importance of Regular Maintenance

Dust and debris in the operating environment can severely impact Spot’s performance. Regular maintenance is critical to:

1. **Prevent Component Failure** – Protect fans, motors, and sensors from overheating or malfunction due to buildup.
2. **Ensure Safe Operation** – Reduce the risk of faults or instability during autonomous or remote missions.
3. **Extend Service Life** – Minimize wear and tear to maximize the robot’s operational lifespan and reliability.

## 📊 Performance Metrics

After completing maintenance, run the following tests to verify that Spot is operating correctly:

### ✅ Post-Maintenance Tests

- **Joint Offset Test**
    
    Use the Boston Dynamics interface to check joint alignment and identify any abnormal joint behavior.
    
- **Basic Mobility Test**
    
    Drive Spot manually to test walking, turning, and stair navigation functions.
    
    - Check for any abnormal movement, hesitation, or unexpected errors.
- **Calibration Test (with Payload)**
    
    Use the **custom calibration board** provided with your payload package:
    
    - Ensure the calibration board is **present on-site** and in good condition.
    - Follow your team’s calibration protocol to align sensors and validate data accuracy.
    - Confirm all payload systems respond accurately after calibration.
- **Sensor and Camera Check**
    
    Review live feeds from all sensors and cameras to ensure clean, accurate visuals and depth sensing.
